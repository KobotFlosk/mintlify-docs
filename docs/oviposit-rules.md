# Can my character use Oviposit?

Check your **true form** first. It must have a vagina, and its species must have **Ovum** births. Female, Andromorph, and Hermaphrodite characters have a vagina. If your true form lacks a vagina or your true species has Live births, you cannot use Oviposit in any form.

If your true form passes both checks, find the form you are wearing in the table. When a species “grants Oviposit,” it gives that particular character or form the ability. A shifted species can have either Live or Ovum births.

| Form you are wearing | When can you use Oviposit? | How do you deposit the eggs? |
|---|---|---|
| Your true form | You can use it if your true species grants Oviposit. | You use your vagina. This also applies if your true form is Hermaphrodite. |
| Shifted Female or Andromorph | You can use it if your shifted species grants Oviposit. Your true species can lack the ability. | You use your shifted form’s vagina. |
| Shifted Male or Gynomorph | You can use it if your true species grants Oviposit. A grant from the shifted species alone cannot enable it. | Your shifted form’s penis acts as an ovipositor. |
| Shifted Hermaphrodite, and your shifted species grants Oviposit | You can use it whether or not your true species also grants it. | You use your shifted form’s vagina, even when both species grant the ability. |
| Shifted Hermaphrodite, and only your true species grants Oviposit | You can use it through your true species’ ability. | Your shifted form’s penis acts as an ovipositor. |
| Shifted Hermaphrodite, and neither species grants Oviposit | You cannot use it in this form. | — |
| Shifted to the gender called None | You cannot use it in this form, even if both species grant it. | — |

You also need access to the body part your current form uses. If it becomes blocked while a deposit is pending, the eggs stay with you.

**Example.** Your true form is Female, has Ovum births, and lacks Oviposit. You learn two forms whose species grants the ability. Wearing the Andromorph form lets you deposit eggs through its vagina. Wearing the Male form leaves Oviposit unavailable because using a shifted penis requires the grant from your true species.

## Which eggs can I deposit?

You can deposit an unfertilized egg only if your character produced it and still carries it. For a pregnancy, your character must be both its mother and its current carrier. Each pregnancy is eligible through 30% of its own gestation. If too few pregnancies are still eligible when the recipient processes your deposit, the whole batch stays with you. If another character deposits an egg into you, it stays out of your Oviposit list. Even if you both have the ability, you cannot pass their egg on to someone else.

To make a deposit, open **Abilities → Oviposit** and choose a character within 10 metres. Choose one of their available openings, then select how many of your eligible eggs or pregnancies to transfer. Stand within 5 metres of the character when you select **Deposit** so your HUD can detect them. If you change to an ineligible form while the transfer is waiting, the eggs stay with you.

## How does a deposit affect my stats?

Only the character receiving the deposit gets these stat changes. They apply once the transfer succeeds. Each unfertilized egg or pregnancy counts as one egg for all three effects.

For every egg you receive through any opening, your **arousal rises by 5 percentage points** (up to 100%) and your **defense falls by 10 percentage points** (down to 0%). That 10-point total includes the usual defense loss from rising arousal.

**Vitality loss from stretching** depends on the width of each egg when it passes through the receiving opening. The sender’s true species determines the egg’s base volume. A fertilized egg grows as its pregnancy progresses. At 20% of gestation, for example, it adds 20% of the offspring species’ maximum embryo volume to that base. The game uses the resulting diameter for each egg in the deposit.

Each opening in your true form has its own width, so the same egg can cause a different loss depending on where you receive it. If only your shifted form has a vagina, an egg deposited there goes to your colon and uses your anal opening’s width.

| Egg width compared with your opening | Share of your remaining vitality lost per egg |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 times as wide | 0% |
| 2 to under 3 times as wide | 10% |
| 3 to under 4 times as wide | 20% |
| 4 to under 5 times as wide | 40% |
| 5 to under 6 times as wide | 80% |
| 6 or more times as wide | 100%. This can be fatal. |

**Example.** You receive two eggs through your mouth, each three times as wide as your mouth opening. Starting at 100% vitality, you drop to 80% after the first egg and 64% after the second. Over the same deposit, 30% arousal rises to 40%, and 70% defense falls to 50%.
