Open **MyProfile → Abilities** to use the abilities available to your character. Eligibility depends on your species, form, and class under the configured ability rules.

See the [Abilities guide](/system-guide/abilities) for Transmute, Bite, target range, and effects.

#### Shift (Form: Shapeshifter)

Transmute lets an eligible character change between learned forms. Shift eligibility is not determined by the shapeshifter form alone. See [Abilities](/system-guide/abilities#which-abilities-you-can-use) for the current rules and [Form shapeshifters](/system-guide/form-shapeshifters) for how a disguise affects your character.
