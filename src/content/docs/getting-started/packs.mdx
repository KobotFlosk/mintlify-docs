A pack is a named group of characters, such as a family or clan. Open the pack option from your character's profile menu to create a pack, view members, invite others, or leave. A pack holds up to **nine characters, including its leader**.

## Roles

- **Alpha** — the pack's single leader. Only the Alpha can invite members or remove another member.
- **Beta** — a second-in-command who can succeed the Alpha. The first invited member becomes the initial Beta.
- **Omega** — a regular member. Later invited members join as Omegas.

<a id="create-"></a>

## Create

Choose **Create** from the pack menu, name your pack, and select a nearby character to found it with. That character becomes the Alpha, with you as the first member. Both players must accept before the pack is created.

## Invite

The Alpha can search nearby characters and send an invitation while the pack has room. The other player can accept or decline.

<a id="members-"></a>

## Members

Open the member list and select a packmate to view their profile. If they are active in-world and the RLV automation feature is enabled, you can teleport to them.

The Alpha can remove other members but cannot remove themselves through that option.

## Leave

Any member can leave. If the Alpha leaves and Betas are available, the Alpha must choose a Beta to take over first. If no Beta is available, the pack dissolves.

## Packs and new births

A newborn automatically joins the birth parent's pack and inherits that parent's role. If the parent is the Alpha, the newborn becomes a Beta instead of replacing the leader.
