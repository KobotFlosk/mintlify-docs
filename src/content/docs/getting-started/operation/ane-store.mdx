## Browse and buy

Open **Shop** from the main menu to see your Credit balance. The balance belongs to your account and is shared across your characters.

Browse the categories to find a product. Folders marked with an asterisk contain more choices. Review the product's description and current Credit cost, then select **Buy**.

If you have enough Credits, the purchase completes and the item is delivered. If you do not, the purchase is refused without a charge. A free item can still be claimed when your balance is negative.

## Claim rewards

Open **Rewards** from the Shop's front page to see unclaimed grants. Rewards can come from events, promotions, or milestones and may contain Credits or a free product.

Selecting a reward claims it immediately. Your balance is credited or delivery of the product is requested. Clearing unclaimed rewards discards them without paying them out.

## Using the browser Store

If your [dashboard](/system-guide/companion-dashboard) offers **Store**, you can review your balance, available products, and unclaimed rewards there. Choose a product and review its current price before purchasing. Products are delivered in-world, not to the browser.

A changed price or insufficient Credits prevents the purchase. Refresh the Store before deciding whether to try again.

Claiming a reward adds its Credits or requests delivery of its product. Clearing unclaimed rewards requires confirmation and **discards them without paying them out**. Already claimed rewards are not cleared.

After an error or interrupted purchase, refresh and check your balance and inventory before starting another purchase. If the outcome is unclear, contact [support](/overview/help-and-support) instead of repeatedly buying the same item. Browser availability and layout depend on your dashboard version.

See [Credit system](/getting-started/operation/credit-system) for other ways to earn and spend Credits.
