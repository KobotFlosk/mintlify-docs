## Browse and buy

Open **Shop** from the main menu to see your Credit balance. The balance belongs to your account and is shared across your characters.

Browse the categories to find a product. Folders marked with an asterisk contain more choices. Review the product's description and current Credit cost, then select **Buy**.

If you have enough Credits, the purchase completes and the item is delivered. If you do not, the purchase is refused without a charge. A free item can still be claimed when your balance is negative.

## Claim rewards

Open **Rewards** from the Shop's front page to see unclaimed grants. Rewards can come from events, promotions, or milestones and may contain Credits or a free product.

Selecting a reward claims it immediately. Your balance is credited or the product is delivered. You can also clear the list of unclaimed rewards.

See [Credit system](/getting-started/operation/credit-system) for other ways to earn and spend Credits.
