The companion web dashboard is an optional browser companion for viewing your character and using supported controls. It is different from the [web portal](/system-guide/web-portal), which handles one-off third-party account connections. Available panels and controls depend on your dashboard version.

## Open the dashboard

The **Web** button opens the test dashboard if you have tester access. Otherwise, it opens the published help. Press **Web** again to close the in-world browser.

If you have tester access, you can also choose **UI** from the **Start** menu before activating a character. This opens the dashboard again instead of toggling it closed. The option disappears if your tester access is removed.

The **Tree** option on a character's profile opens that character's separate web view. If an opening link expires, open the view again from the companion.

## Closing the view and opening outside links

Where available, the dashboard's close control closes the in-world browser. Opening an outside link asks for your approval in a separate viewer prompt. It does not replace the dashboard or discard your pending scene choice.

Review the destination before accepting. If no prompt arrives, keep the companion worn and let it reconnect before trying again.

## Dashboard panels

The dashboard can show:

- **Live status** — whether the companion is active and, during a scene, a live narration of what is happening.
- **Character sheet** — identity, species, form, class, birthplace, parents, stats, fertility, pregnancy, and body growth.
- **Abilities** — available actions such as Transmute (Shift) and Bite, with the same controls as the in-world menu. See [Abilities](/system-guide/abilities).
- **Character details** — trusted characters, partners, family, species, pregnancies, and histories. You can remove an existing trust or dissolve a partnership. Dissolving a partnership removes it for both characters. Nearby partnership invitations and character-specific species overrides still use the in-world menus.
- **Genetics, pregnancies, and births** — visibility depends on your character. See [Viewing character histories](/system-guide/reproduction-and-genetics#viewing-character-histories). Pregnancy progress and estimated delivery time appear from 10% development. Delivery remains in-world.
- **Species settings** — if you own a species, you can edit its shared base settings after acknowledging the impact on other characters. See [Editing a species you own](/system-guide/creating-a-species#editing-a-species-you-own).
- **Items and devices** — nearby usable items and management controls for worn devices.
- **Attachments** — add or remove managed products and switch a supported attachment to manual **Override** to use its controls yourself.
- **Settings** — account preferences, privacy controls, movement or RLV restrictions, and access permissions.
- **Search and discovery** — nearby, online, or fertile characters with compatibility information and interaction controls.
- **World presence** — community activity, busy regions, and common species.
- **Current location** — your latest reported region, separate from your character's birthplace. This can appear before you activate a character.
- **Store** — your balance, available products, purchases, reward claims, and clearing unclaimed rewards. See [Using the browser Store](/getting-started/operation/ane-store#using-the-browser-store).

## Answering scene choices

You can answer a supported scene prompt in the browser or in-world. Both views represent the same choice. The first accepted answer advances the scene, and you cannot answer an old prompt again. Choices, confirmations, and short text replies are supported.

Each partner can have a separate pending choice. Check the partner before answering. Answering one prompt does not consume another partner's choice.

Use the current prompt if an earlier one expires or is replaced. Refresh the browser to recover a still-pending choice. If a text reply is rejected, try a shorter reply. Opening another in-world menu can dismiss the scene prompt; touch the companion again to return to it.

Where the dashboard offers an **emergency stop**, it ends only the selected engagement, and only if your character initiated it. You can use it while waiting for a turn or while tied. Other engagements remain active. See [Stopping an engagement](/system-guide/interaction-and-roleplay#stopping-an-engagement).

## Changing characters and handling stale views

You can leave your active character from the dashboard while idle, then choose another character. Finish any active scene or delivery first.

If you switched characters or someone changed species settings while a page was open, refresh before trying again. An outdated page cannot apply those changes.

## Staying in sync

The dashboard uses recent companion activity to show whether you are present. Keep the companion worn when using live controls. If it appears offline after removal or a region change, wait for it to reconnect and refresh.

## Privacy and security

Open your own web view from the companion instead of sharing opening links. Review confirmations carefully, especially when dissolving a partnership or changing a species used by other people.

## Control attachments manually

Open **Attachments** to see the products your companion manages and the available add/remove controls. Turn on **Override** for an attachment to pause its automatic reactions and use its individual controls or test commands. Manual controls require Override to be enabled.

Turn **Override** off when you want automatic reactions to resume. See [Third-party integrations](/system-guide/third-party-integrations#manual-attachment-controls).
