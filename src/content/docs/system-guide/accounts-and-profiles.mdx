title: "Accounts and profiles"
description: "Understand the difference between your account and characters, and manage relationships, trust, roles, and settings."
---

Your account represents you in the virtual world. Your character profile represents the persona you play. One account can own several characters, but only one character can be active at a time.

## Account vs. character

- Your **account** is created automatically the first time you use the companion. It stores information shared by all your characters, including unlocked rewards, special roles, and your active character.
- A **character**, also called a profile, stores an in-character persona's name, species, body, class, stats, relationships, and history.

## Create and switch characters

- The companion guides you through creating your first character the first time you wear it.
- From the main menu, you can create more characters when your account allows it, switch your active character, or resume an existing character.
- You can also assume a character from a nearby egg or another hand-off. This is how you can begin playing a newly born character.

## Optional character identity

Where your dashboard offers **Character → Profile → Change**, you can save a
sexuality and character picture for your active character. The sexuality choices
are Straight, Gay, Lesbian, Bi, Pan, Asexual, Demisexual, and Questioning; leaving
it unspecified is also supported. These are character identity details, not
changes to reproductive biology or breeding compatibility.

These details belong to the character, so changing appearance through
shapeshifting does not replace them. They can appear in the character overview
and other people's discovery results: only save details you intend to share.
Leaving a detail blank clears it.

If you change characters while editing, refresh before saving. If another edit
has already changed the same details, reload the latest values rather than
overwriting them from an old view. A rejected picture or sexuality leaves both
details unchanged. A picture that saves successfully may still be unavailable
to display.

## Relationships and trust

- You can link characters as **partners**. The relationship appears for both characters automatically.
- You can mark another character as **trusted**. Trust can relax certain interaction or exchange limits between your characters.
- Parent and child links can result from adoption or birth. These relationships affect genetics and ancestry.

Adoptions can also establish **Master/Slave** or **Owner/Pet** bonds. Trust, partnership, and certain ownership bonds can cause breeding requests to auto-accept. See [When a breeding request auto-accepts](/system-guide/interaction-and-roleplay#when-a-breeding-request-auto-accepts) for all conditions and their priority.

## Packs

Characters can form a named pack with an Alpha, optional Betas, and Omegas. See [Packs](/getting-started/packs) for the full walkthrough.

## Special roles and permissions

Some accounts receive extra roles for moderation, private testing, or access to experimental features such as the AI companion. These roles are granted individually and do not change the regular player experience.

## Settings

Accounts and characters have separate settings for notification preferences, content limits, and feature toggles. You can change them from **Settings** on the main menu.

## Professions

A special consumable can grant your character a profession title, such as **Chemist** or **Doctor**. These titles are recorded on your character as cosmetic badges; they do not grant a gameplay bonus.

## Blocking

You can block another person from your nearby, online, and fertile character results. Blocking affects only what you see: it does not notify them or prevent them from finding or contacting you. Name-based profile searches and region activity totals are not hidden by this block. See [Search and discovery](/system-guide/search-and-discovery).
