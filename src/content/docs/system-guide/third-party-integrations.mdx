You can connect optional online services and wear compatible products alongside AnE. Choose the connections you want from your account settings.

## Connected services

| Connection | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Messaging bridge** | Notifications through a linked messaging account, with optional chat relay when you are away from the virtual world. |
| **Connected-toy bridge** | A supported toy can respond to events during a scene. |
| **Character directory** | Links an external character-profile directory so shared preferences can inform scene options and character information can be shared back. |
| **Game platform** | Links your game-platform identity for features such as friend lookups. |

These connections are optional. Complete the authorization or pairing steps shown for the service. You can turn the connections off from your settings.

When a connection needs a browser step, the [web portal](/system-guide/web-portal) explains what you are linking and confirms the result.

## Compatible body add-ons and gadgets

AnE recognizes supported mesh body parts, fluid effects, reactive vocal add-ons, touch-reactive systems, and compatible mini-games. Wearing a supported product alongside the companion lets it respond to relevant events such as touches, climaxes, or births.

Support depends on the product. Wearing an arbitrary accessory does not make it compatible with AnE.
