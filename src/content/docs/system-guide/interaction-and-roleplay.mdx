How the companion comes to life, how shared intimate scenes are driven, and how role-play flows through a scene.

## Waking up and the main menu

When you attach the companion it "wakes up" and, if this is your first time, walks you through creating a character. After that, touching it opens an interactive menu where you reach your profile, settings, searches, the store, help, and any add-ons you own. Between actions it sits in a calm, idle mode, quietly keeping your character's biology and stats up to date.

## Being greeted and staying in the loop

When you attach or reset the companion, or move between regions, it greets you by name. It reminds you which characters are fertile or pregnant and relays active community announcements. Seasonal sounds or appearances are cosmetic and do not change gameplay.

## Starting a scene with a partner

When another user of the system is nearby, you can begin a shared scene together. The companion looks at **both** characters — their bodies, forms, and the personal limits each of you set — and only offers actions that make sense for the two of you. Scenes are organised into kinds of intimacy (for example oral, penetrative, breeding, and egg-laying), each with its own set of moves.

## When a breeding request auto-accepts

You normally receive a prompt to **accept**, **reject**, or **trust** a breeding request. The following conditions can accept a request automatically without that prompt. If several apply, the companion announces one reason in this priority order:

1. **Unable to refuse** — your character has insufficient strength, defense, or stamina to defend themselves.
2. **Public access** — you enabled public access in your settings.
3. **Partnership** — you are partnered with the requester.
4. **Trust** — you marked the requester as trusted.
5. **Ownership bond** — the requester is your **Master**, your **Owner**, or your **Pet**.

If no condition applies, you receive the normal prompt. These are in-character breeding mechanics. See [Accounts and profiles](/system-guide/accounts-and-profiles) for trust and relationship setup, and [Vitality and stats](/system-guide/vitality-and-stats) for the stats involved.

## During a scene

- You pick moves from a menu; your partner sees and responds to what happens.
- Actions affect your **stats** — arousal builds, stamina drains, and so on.
- Reaching a climax can transfer **fluids** between characters, which is what makes conception possible (see [Reproduction and genetics](/system-guide/reproduction-and-genetics)).
- If you have add-on devices or an AI companion enabled, they react in real time — narrating, buzzing a connected toy, or triggering special effects.
- Use **Clothing** to strip or redress outfit pieces without leaving the scene. With your partner's permission, you can also manage their clothing. The **Partner** menu provides shortcuts to supported clothing and connected toys.
- You can stop the scene and return to idle mode. Initial breeding requests may auto-accept under the conditions above; do not rely on receiving an acceptance prompt every time.

## Choosing how turns are paced

Set **Stories → Interaction** to choose how ordinary physical scenes proceed:

- **Free** — the initiator can take consecutive actions, and the partner can respond when a move is directed at them without enforced turn waiting.
- **Turn** — you alternate turns and wait for the other person to respond.
- **Timeout** — you alternate turns, but a turn passes automatically if there is no response within the selected preset time.

The initiator's preference applies to that scene. A changed preference applies to the next scene you start. AI-narrated stories always alternate turns and do not use this setting.

## Your limits are always in charge

Everything offered during a scene is filtered by your body and by the content limits you configure. If something isn't possible or isn't allowed for your character, it simply won't be offered.
